# Audio diagnostics archive

Retired after the internal per-note FluidSynth channel/tuning-map redesign
eliminated playback crackling in device listening tests. This preserves capture,
WAV export, synthetic tests, the canvas MIDI trace and the instrumented worklet
and bridge as reference material. These snapshots are not production entry points.

The bridge and worklet snapshots retain their original relative imports; restore
them to their original locations before using them. Existing audio diagnostics
tests are archived alongside their corresponding helpers.
